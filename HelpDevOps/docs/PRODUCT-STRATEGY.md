# Product Strategy

## Users
DevOps, cloud, SRE, platform and infrastructure engineers; secondarily learners and career switchers.

## Differentiation
- Consistent DevOps workspace
- Privacy-first local processing
- Transparent validation and limitations
- Reduced context switching
- DevOps Preflight flagship

## Feature Gate
A feature should solve a real problem, save time, be trustworthy, support repeat use, fit browser-side architecture, strengthen the workspace and remain maintainable.

## Locked long-term identity

HelpDevOps helps engineers find the safest, evidence-backed next step whenever they are stuck. It should evolve into a vendor-neutral DevOps problem-solving platform rather than a generic blog, content farm, AI wrapper, observability system, or command-execution platform.

The business direction is mission-first free public value, global organic traffic, restrained future AdSense, and ethical contextual affiliates. Critical troubleshooting guidance must not be paywalled or biased by commercial relationships.
