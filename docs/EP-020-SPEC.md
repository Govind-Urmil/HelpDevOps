# EP-020 Product Experience, Investigation Intelligence, Diagnostic Expansion & Launch Certification

## Objective

Make HelpDevOps faster to understand and safer to use during real operational pressure while preserving its static, deterministic, browser-local architecture.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Homepage and troubleshooting discovery begin from observable symptoms.
- Journeys state time, depth, review date, risk, limitations, and ranked cause candidates without claiming probability.
- Seven high-value investigations cover Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, Linux, Git, Jenkins, and AWS with three evidence-based branches each.
- Read-only checks precede mutation; owner, rollback, verification, prevention, and escalation remain explicit.
- Terminal states produce a concise investigation or escalation summary.
- Accessibility, responsive behavior, source encoding, security, performance budgets, and release packaging remain certified by the existing validation system.

## Non-goals

No backend, telemetry, runtime asset fetch, AI diagnosis, framework replacement, broad redesign, or production deployment.
