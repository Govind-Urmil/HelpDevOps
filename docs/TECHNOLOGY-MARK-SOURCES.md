# Technology mark sources and usage

HelpDevOps stores all technology marks locally under `public/icons/technologies/`; no runtime asset request is made to a vendor or CDN. The SVGs are small, decorative, carry a `<title>` for source inspection, and are rendered with an empty `alt` beside a visible technology label to avoid duplicate accessible names.

These are lightweight HelpDevOps renditions for nominative product identification, using recognizable geometry and vendor brand colors. Product names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners and do not imply endorsement.

Do not replace these files with hot-linked vendor assets. Re-review vendor guidance before materially changing a mark or using it outside the HelpDevOps interface.
